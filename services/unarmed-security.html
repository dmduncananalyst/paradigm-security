Unarmed Security

Unarmed officers work in settings where people arrive for business, shopping, appointments, residential visits or events. Their presence can provide a recognizable point of contact for security concerns while supporting the normal use of the property. Professional interaction matters because the officer may be one of the first people a visitor encounters.

Security That Fits Everyday Property Operations

Paradigm supports clients who need lobby security, visitor management, common area observation or a consistent security contact for employees and tenants. A residential community has different expectations from an office reception area. The service is defined around those differences rather than treating every property as the same assignment.

Access Management and Property Observation

Responsibilities can include visitor sign in, verification of authorized entry, observation of common areas and communication with the client when concerns arise. Officers can assist with the property’s existing visitor process and help direct people to the appropriate contact. The client’s rules and the officer’s assigned responsibilities establish the boundaries of that work.

Property observation is another part of unarmed security. Officers can identify and report disturbances, unauthorized access concerns or conditions requiring management attention. The role is not to act as maintenance staff or make decisions reserved for the property owner. It is to provide attentive security support and communicate what needs attention.

Incident Communication and Reporting

When an incident occurs, clear information helps the client understand what was observed and what actions were taken. Paradigm includes incident documentation and reporting among its unarmed security services. That record can support management follow up and communication with the appropriate outside responders when necessary.

Unarmed security should not be confused with police authority or a promise that every disturbance can be resolved by one officer. The assignment establishes reporting responsibilities and the appropriate point of contact. BSIS publishes California security guard registration and training requirements. The official guidance provides a reference for the regulatory process without substituting for confirmation of individual credentials.

Unarmed Security Across Both Counties

Paradigm serves property managers, businesses and event organizers in Los Angeles County and San Bernardino County. Unarmed coverage may be relevant to a single office building, a residential property, a shopping location or an event where professional interaction and access responsibilities are central to the assignment.

Contact Paradigm with a description of your site and the concerns you want addressed. We will review the duties required, the relationship with your management team and whether unarmed coverage fits the assignment. If your needs also involve loss prevention, mobile patrol or another service, those responsibilities can be considered separately during the consultation.
