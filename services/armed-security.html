Armed Security

Armed security can support financial institutions, licensed cannabis businesses, commercial properties, residential communities and private clients. Each setting has different expectations. A public facing business needs an officer who can interact professionally with customers. A residential assignment requires respect for household privacy. An event brings additional responsibilities involving guests, organizers and venue personnel.

Protection for People and Property

Paradigm reviews those expectations with the client before confirming an assignment. The conversation establishes the purpose of the post and the duties the officer will perform. The goal is a clear service relationship rather than a generic promise that every armed officer will handle every security need.

Responsibilities Beyond a Visible Presence

An armed security assignment can include access management, observation of property conditions, support when disturbances occur and communication with the person responsible for the site. Officers can document incidents and relay relevant information to management. Where police or other emergency services are needed, the security role includes communicating concerns and supporting the appropriate response.

The exact responsibilities depend on the property and the agreement. Premises security, personal protection and transport related security are distinct assignments. Selecting armed coverage does not automatically include all three. Paradigm identifies the agreed responsibilities so clients understand what support is being provided and where another service may be necessary.

Armed Security Within Its Proper Role

Private security serves the client and the assigned property. It does not replace police, emergency medical services or the decisions of facility management. An officer is expected to act within the authority relevant to the situation and the responsibilities of the assignment. A firearm does not turn a private security position into a law enforcement position.

California armed security work involves an applicable registration or license and a separate BSIS firearms permit. Clients can consult the official permit guidance for the requirements that apply to the individual performing armed duties. Specific qualifications and assignment suitability should be confirmed during the consultation.

For clients seeking a discreet appearance, Concealed Weapon Detail can be reviewed as a separate assignment requirement. A BSIS exposed firearms permit does not itself authorize concealed carry. Any concealed firearm assignment requires the applicable additional authorization, and availability is confirmed with Paradigm.
