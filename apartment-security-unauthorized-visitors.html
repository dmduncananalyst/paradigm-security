Security Options for Apartment Buildings With Unauthorized Visitors

Residents may become concerned when unfamiliar people regularly enter an apartment garage, lobby or other restricted common area. Property managers must balance resident access with the need to protect shared spaces.

Access control systems, functioning gates and adequate lighting can support property security. When unauthorized entry continues, a professional security presence may provide additional monitoring and incident reporting.

A security assignment can focus on shared entrances, parking areas or other locations identified by management. Guards need clear instructions regarding authorized access, visitor policies and when to contact management or law enforcement.

Residents should have a safe way to report concerns without confronting unfamiliar individuals. Reporting should focus on specific behavior and access violations rather than appearance or assumptions about a person.
