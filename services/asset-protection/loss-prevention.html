Loss Prevention

Loss prevention differs from general premises security because its focus is the retail environment and theft related concerns. A store may already have security for its entrance or shopping center common areas while needing separate support inside the business. Those responsibilities should be identified rather than assumed to be part of every guard assignment.

Retail Security With a Specific Purpose

Paradigm works with retailers ranging from individual storefronts to businesses with multiple locations. The service can address concerns involving merchandise, suspected shoplifting and incidents affecting employees or customers. It is not a promise that every inventory discrepancy is theft or that a security officer can explain every source of lost stock.

Uniformed and Plainclothes Assignments

Uniformed loss prevention provides an identifiable security presence in the store. Plainclothes loss prevention offers a different presentation for assignments where a visible uniform is not the chosen approach. Both forms have a retail security purpose, and both require clear responsibilities and coordination with the client.

The selection is reviewed in relation to the business, the concerns being reported and the responsibilities of the assignment. The public description does not disclose observation methods or store specific security procedures. Those details belong in the private service assessment. What matters publicly is that the retailer understands the service purpose and the relationship between loss prevention and other security roles.

Observation, Reporting and Store Coordination

Loss prevention personnel can document observations and incidents and coordinate with store management. When police involvement is appropriate, the service can support communication of relevant information. Records should distinguish what was observed from assumptions about the cause of an inventory loss or the conduct of a customer or employee.

The service operates within the retailer’s policies and applicable authority. A loss prevention assignment is not a general grant of police powers. BSIS publishes California security guard registration and training requirements. Questions involving detention authority or other legal limits belong in the relevant legal guidance and the agreed responsibilities of the assignment.

Support for Retailers Across Both Counties

Paradigm supports retailers in Los Angeles County and San Bernardino County. Your inquiry may involve recurring suspected theft, a need for uniformed presence or a need to distinguish premises security from dedicated loss prevention. The consultation provides an opportunity to explain the concern without publishing sensitive store information.

Contact Paradigm to review the retail setting and the support you need. We will identify whether loss prevention is the appropriate service, explain the available presentation and clarify communication with your management team. Where other concerns involve parking areas, shared property or general access, those can be considered separately so the service scope remains clear.
