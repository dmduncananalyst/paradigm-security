Resident Threats and Responsibility for Apartment Common Areas

A dispute with a neighbor can become a concern about using the building's shared spaces. A resident may feel unable to reach the laundry room, parking area, or lobby without encountering the person involved. That situation combines personal conduct, access to common areas, and property management responsibilities. It is more substantial than a general complaint that two residents do not get along.
