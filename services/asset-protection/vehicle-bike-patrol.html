Vehicle and Bike Patrol

Vehicle patrol can support business parks, parking areas, campuses and properties containing several buildings. It allows the security role to extend beyond a single lobby or entrance. Paradigm’s existing service includes marked vehicle patrol as an identifiable presence on the property. The assignment can involve observation, communication with management and documentation of incidents.

Vehicle Patrol for Larger Properties

Mobile coverage is not the same service as an officer assigned continuously to one entrance. Clients should understand that distinction when reviewing responsibilities. The description does not promise a specific arrival time or imply that one patrol unit is continuously present at every part of a property.

Bike Patrol for Pedestrian Settings

Bike patrol is relevant to pedestrian areas such as shopping centers, campuses and event venues. It provides a mobile officer presence in a setting where the assignment may involve interaction with shoppers, staff, visitors or event participants. The service is considered in relation to the site and the duties required.

An outdoor retail center has different expectations from a private event or institutional campus. The same method of mobility does not make those assignments identical. Paradigm reviews the people using the property, the areas included in the service and the relationship with management so that the role is understood before coverage begins.

Observation and Property Communication

Patrol personnel can observe property conditions, respond to reported concerns within the assignment and communicate relevant information to the client. Incident documentation provides a record of observations and actions. Where an issue needs police, emergency services or maintenance attention, the security role supports communication with the appropriate contact.

Patrol personnel do not replace the property manager or become law enforcement officers by using a marked vehicle. BSIS publishes California security guard registration and training requirements. The private security role remains tied to the agreed assignment and applicable authority. Patrol patterns and other operational details are not published on this service page.
