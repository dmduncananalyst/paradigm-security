Individuals and Executives

Personal security may be requested because of threats, harassment, public exposure or a need for support during business and personal activities. The client does not have to fit a single public image for the service to be reviewed. Paradigm considers inquiries from private individuals as well as executives and public figures.

Protection Focused on the Individual

The role is defined around the person and the circumstances they want addressed. A meeting related assignment differs from coverage at a residence or a public appearance. The consultation clarifies those needs without turning the client’s concerns, identity or personal movements into public information.

Professional Presence Without Unnecessary Disruption

Personal protection can involve plainclothes personnel where that presentation is suitable to the assignment. The purpose is to support the client’s security needs while recognizing the social and professional settings in which the service will operate. Presentation alone does not define the scope or guarantee that the service will be invisible.

Paradigm reviews expectations with the client or authorized representative. The service can relate to meetings, public appearances, residence concerns or travel within the agreed coverage area. Transportation related support and personal protection are not assumed to include every vehicle or travel service unless those responsibilities are specifically confirmed.

A Clear Relationship With Other Security Roles

Personal protection differs from a general premises guard assignment. A property officer may be responsible for an entrance or shared space, while the personal protection role is connected with the individual. Both services may be relevant to the same situation, but their responsibilities should remain understandable.

The service also does not replace police involvement in reported threats or the advice of legal professionals. Security personnel can support communication and work within the agreed assignment. BSIS publishes California security guard registration requirements. The suitability of personnel and any armed duties are confirmed in relation to the proposed personal protection service.

Personal Protection in Los Angeles and San Bernardino Counties

Paradigm serves clients throughout Los Angeles County and San Bernardino County. The inquiry may concern a private individual, an executive, a public figure or an organization arranging support for an authorized person. The consultation focuses on the requested protection and the settings in which the service is needed.

Contact Paradigm to review your concerns privately. We will clarify the service available, the presentation of personnel and the responsibilities included in the assignment. Personal information and sensitive movement details remain within that conversation. The public website explains the service without publishing individual protection arrangements or promising a particular outcome.
